Eating out

Pappy & Harriet's

Sun 9/20 · Lunch
Legendary BBQ saloon out in the desert — mesquite-grilled ribs and tri-tip, mac and cheese people can't stop raving about, and a genuine old-West, live-music vibe. Get the ribs.

Sky High Pie

Tue 9/22 · Lunch
Sourdough pizza right outside the park entrance. The Diavola and Rise N' Shine pies get all the love, the tiramisu is a sleeper hit, and there's a soft-serve window for after. Depending how busy the day is, we can sit down there or just bring pies back to the house.

Tacos Los Oaxacos

Sat 9/19 · Lunch
Local taco spot in Joshua Tree — good timing since we'll probably be out exploring the park around then anyway.

The Dez

Wed 9/23 · Lunch
Tiny takeaway counter in town, great for a post-hike refuel — fresh sandwiches and paninis, Buddha bowls, good coffee. The Caprese panini's a favorite.

Hiking Trail Details

Short loops, about an hour or under. Drive times are rough estimates from the house.

01

Hidden Valley Nature Trail

~1 mi ~45 min hike ~30 min drive

Easy loop through a boulder-ringed valley, good before it heats up. Saturday's scheduled hike.

02

Barker Dam Loop

1.1 mi 45–60 min hike ~30 min drive

Easy loop through the boulders past a historic dam.

03

Skull Rock Trail

1.7 mi ~1 hr hike ~35 min drive

Easy loop that swings by the park's namesake rock formation.

04

Arch Rock Trail

1.8 mi ~1 hr hike ~40 min drive

Easy walk out to a natural rock arch, with some scrambling if you want it.

Viewpoint Details

No real hiking — just short walks from the parking area. Best at sunset. Drive times are rough estimates from the house.

01

Cholla Cactus Garden

~0.25 mi loop ~15 min walk ~55 min drive

Short, flat, self-guided loop through a dense field of backlit teddy-bear cholla — glows gold at sunset. Watch your step (and kids/pets) around the spines.

02

Keys View

steps from parking no hike required ~30 min drive

Paved overlook at 5,185 ft with panoramic views across the Coachella Valley to the Salton Sea, plus the San Andreas Fault. Can get windy and cool — bring a layer.
